Portfolio Health

Reported vs. Computed Health

Reported Health is the red/yellow/green status selected by the Project Manager or manager in Confluence and remains the official status. Computed Health is an independent, rule-based signal calculated from structured ratings (0–4 across 12 weighted dimensions). It supports management review and never changes Reported Health.

How to read this section
  • Reported = the status the project team chose in Confluence. Computed = a score calculated from 12 rated areas. They are never merged.
  • Score: 0–100. ✔ Green 85+▲ Yellow 70–84◆ Orange 55–69✖ Red under 55○ Incomplete
  • Alignment flags where the two disagree. Hidden Risk = reported Green but computed Orange/Red. A difference means “take a look”; it never changes the reported status.
  • Incomplete means some areas have not been rated yet. Missing data is never counted as zero.
  • Start with Alignment Review, click a count to filter the table, then click a project name for the full detail.
