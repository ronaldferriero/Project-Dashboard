Time to Value by Level

Time to value (TTV) is implementation start to go-live, in months, for completed projects only. Go-lives pinned to Dec 31 are treated as placeholders and excluded. Remaining time is the estimated go-live minus today for active projects in the level.

Active Projects vs. Peers

Estimated go-live is the start date plus the median TTV of the project's level (or the whole portfolio when the level has fewer than 5 completed projects). Projects already past that point use the level's 75th percentile, with at least 2 months remaining. "Plan vs peers" is the planned duration minus the peer median; Aggressive means the plan is shorter than 75% of peers, Padded means longer than 75% of peers.

How Levels Are Assigned

Level (1–3) is the number of core modules contracted: Community Development, Business Management and Environmental Health. A project always has at least one, so the lowest level is 1. Client or city population is not used. See the Category Guide for the exact cutoffs, product lists and examples.
