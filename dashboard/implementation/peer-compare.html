Benchmark

The benchmark is the group of completed projects that a category's time-to-value is compared against. A group needs at least 5 completed projects to count.

  • Blank: the category has enough completed projects of its own, so its own median is used.
  • “uses Medium” (or Low / High): the category has fewer than 5, so the median for its whole tier is used instead.
  • “uses All projects”: the tier is also too small, so the median for every completed project is used.

The benchmark sets each active project's estimated go-live (start date plus the benchmark's median TTV) and the Plan vs peers flags (Aggressive or Padded).

Active Projects vs. Peers

Estimated go-live is the start date plus the median TTV of the project's tier (or the whole portfolio when the tier has fewer than 5 completed projects). Projects already past that point use the tier's 75th percentile, with at least 2 months remaining. "Plan vs peers" is the planned duration minus the peer median; Aggressive means the plan is shorter than 75% of peers, Padded means longer than 75% of peers.

How Levels Are Assigned

Every project gets a label like Medium 2: a tier (Low / Medium / High) for contract size, and a level (1–3) for how many core modules were bought. Projects are only compared with others in the same group.

Level (1–3): core modules contracted

Core modules are Community Development, Business Management and Environmental Health. Level 1 = one core module, Level 2 = two, Level 3 = all three. If none is listed, Level 1 is assumed.

Tier (Low / Medium / High): contract value

For now the tier is based on contract value only (Services + SaaS):

Unclassified means the project has no usable contract value, so it can't be given a tier. Enter the contract value on the list and it will get one.

Client or city population is not used. Hover a tier badge to see the reasons behind it. A manual override shows as the label you set. See the Category Guide for product lists and examples.
