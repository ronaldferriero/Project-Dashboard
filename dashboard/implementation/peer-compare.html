How to read this section

Go-live year: January 1 to December 31. Go-lives pinned to Dec 31 (placeholders) are left out.

Start date: the SharePoint list's start date when there is one. Otherwise the more recent of the Confluence page's start date and the start-date worksheet (earliest PSA time entry; marked “(actual)”). A completed project with no start date anywhere is given a 12 month timeline (marked “(assumed)”).

Outliers: projects far outside that year's typical range (more than 1.5 times the middle half beyond it), such as one 50-month project among projects near 13 months. The median barely moves for them; the average does.

Over target: completed projects at or above 16 months (Low and Medium tier, and projects with no tier) or 20 months (High tier), with the share of that year's go-lives. This is separate from outliers.

Avg TTV excl. on-hold clients (2025 onward) drops projects whose client was On Hold in any history snapshot, matched on account number. History starts in June 2026, so earlier hold periods are not captured.

The bottom row covers every project: counts are totals and the TTV figures are calculated across all of them, not averaged from the rows above.

What the Benchmark column means

The benchmark is the group of completed projects a category is compared against. A group needs at least 5 completed projects to count.

  • Blank: the category has enough completed projects of its own.
  • “uses Medium” (or Low / High): the category has fewer than 5, so its whole tier is used.
  • “uses All projects”: the tier is also too small, so every completed project is used.

The benchmark sets each active project's estimated go-live (start plus the benchmark's median TTV) and the Aggressive / Padded flags.

Active Projects vs. Peers

Estimated go-live is the start date plus the median TTV of the project's benchmark. Projects already past that point use the 75th percentile, with at least 2 months remaining. “Plan vs peers” is planned months minus the peer median: Aggressive is shorter than 75% of peers, Padded is longer than 75% of peers.

How Categories Are Assigned

Every project gets a label like Medium 2: a tier (Low / Medium / High) for contract size and a level (1–3) for how many core modules were bought. Projects are only compared with others in the same group.

Level: core modules contracted

Core modules are Community Development, Business Management and Environmental Health. Level 1 = one, Level 2 = two, Level 3 = all three. If none is listed, Level 1 is assumed.

Tier: contract value
